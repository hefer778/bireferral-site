Frequently asked questions

Who is BI Referral for?

VARs and systems integrators selling to technology executives in the Midwest and Southern USA.

How do referral campaigns work?

You choose a monthly campaign plan, and we introduce your team to technology executives in our member network by email, through virtual meetings, or in person at member events. Every plan also includes events we run for you.

Which campaign plan is right for us?

Bronze covers monthly email outreach, an annual co-hosted webinar and one Roundtable event a year. Silver adds virtual meetings over Teams or Zoom, quarterly account reviews and two Executive Evening events a year. Gold adds in-person contact at member events, executives' personal contact details, a dedicated account manager and one Signature event a year. Email sales@bireferral.com and we'll help you choose.

Can we book events without a campaign plan?

Yes. VARs and integrators can start with a single event. The Roundtable, Executive Evening and Signature formats can all be booked on their own.

Will we receive executives' personal contact details?

Gold clients receive the personal contact details of the executives they're introduced to. To protect our members' trust, Bronze and Silver clients don't receive personally identifiable information unless the individual member gives express consent.

Who are the executives in your network?

Technology executives in the Midwest and Southern USA. Our network was built over three decades, and membership is by invitation only: a new member must be referred by at least two existing members.

What kinds of events can you organize for us?

We offer three event formats: the Roundtable, a breakfast or lunch at an exclusive golf and country club for about 10 guests; the Executive Evening, a private evening for about 20; and Signature, a half-day or full-evening experience for about 40. We handle the venue, hospitality, invitations and logistics, whether you're a VAR, an integrator or a partnership of both.

How do we get started?

Email sales@bireferral.com with a little about your business, whether you're a VAR, an integrator or both, and the executives you'd like to meet. We'll recommend a campaign plan or an event.
